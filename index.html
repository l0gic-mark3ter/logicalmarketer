LET’S CLEAR A FEW THINGS UP.

A few things
you might be wondering.

What’s included in the $750?

A business website, Google Business Profile optimization, and initial basic SEO setup for your website. We’ll agree on the details before starting. Ongoing SEO work is not included.

What’s the difference between basic and ongoing SEO?

The basic setup helps Google understand your website when it launches: what you do, where you work, and how your pages are organized. Ongoing SEO is continued work over time, such as adding content and making improvements. That is available through a separate plan.

What if I already have a website?

We’ll take a look at it and your Google listing, then explain what we’d improve.

Are there other costs?

Any separate costs, including hosting and your domain name, will be explained before you agree to the project. Ongoing support and long-term marketing plans are optional and priced separately.

Do I have to pay for ads?

No. This package focuses on your website and unpaid Google search results. Advertising is a separate option.

Can you promise a certain number of leads?

No. Local demand, competition, reviews, and other factors affect results. We focus on helping the right people find your business and making it easier for them to contact you.

Can you work on my Google Business Profile?

Your business needs to meet Google’s eligibility requirements, and we’ll need access to its profile. Google handles verification and approval.
